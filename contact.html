Quick answers

Common support questions.

How do I amend or cancel a transfer?

Contact support immediately with your reference number. Edits or cancellations are generally only possible before the receiving partner completes payout.

Why was additional ID verification requested?

Regulated remittance providers periodically verify identity documents or source-of-funds statements to comply with regional KYC and AML laws.

How long do cancelled transfer refunds take?

Refund timing depends on your original funding rail, typically returning to bank accounts or cards within 2–5 business days after cancellation confirmation.
