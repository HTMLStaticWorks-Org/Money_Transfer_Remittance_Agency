Smart timing

Practical FX tips before sending.

Check weekday market hours

Global currency markets close over weekends, so some providers widen rate buffers on Saturdays and Sundays.

Bundle smaller transfers when fees are flat

If a corridor charges a fixed transfer fee, sending fewer larger transfers can reduce total fee percentage.

Verify rate-lock expiry times
