Fraud prevention

Safe transfer habits vs. common scam red flags.

Safe practices

Do verify before sending

  • Confirm recipient identity through a trusted phone number you already know.
  • Verify exact branch, IBAN, or wallet details directly with your family or supplier.
  • Keep your reference code (MTCN) confidential between you and your recipient.
Warning signs

Watch out for scam red flags

  • Requests to wire money for fake job fees, lottery winnings, or emergency impersonation.
  • Pressure to pay immediately using gift cards, crypto, or unverified cash pickup agents.
  • Someone coaching you to lie about the purpose of your transfer during compliance checks.
