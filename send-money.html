Checklist

Before confirmation

Review every element of a quote with your licensed provider before authorizing payment.

  • Total sending amount: Confirm base currency debit and any card surcharge.
  • Exchange rate & fee: Verify the exact rate and whether the fee is deducted or added.
  • Recipient payout: Check net amount arriving in destination currency.
  • Delivery estimate: Confirm local bank holiday or cutoff windows.
  • Rate lock expiry: Know how long your quoted rate remains valid prior to funding.

Destination requirements

Recipient details needed by region.

India

Recipient full name, bank account number, and 11-character IFSC code (or verified UPI ID).

Europe & UK

Full legal name, IBAN for SEPA destinations, or 6-digit Sort Code and Account Number for UK.

Philippines & SEA

Bank account details, mobile wallet phone number, or matching government ID name for cash pickup.

Americas

18-digit CLABE for Mexico SPEI bank deposits, or ACH routing and account numbers in North America.

Verification tiers

Typical transfer limits overview.

Actual transaction thresholds depend on local regulations and identity verification status.

  • Standard Verified: Everyday family support with basic government ID verification.
  • Enhanced Verified: Higher limits unlocked with proof of address and source of funds.
  • Business Treasury: Custom monthly thresholds following corporate KYB review.
How long is an exchange rate typically held?

Most providers hold a quoted rate for a fixed window (for example 15 minutes to 24 hours) until your payment clears.

What happens if a recipient name does not match their bank account?

Mismatched names or invalid routing codes can delay settlement or trigger an automatic return minus intermediary fees.

Can I edit or cancel a transfer after sending?

Amendments or cancellations are typically only possible before payout settlement completes with the partner rail.
